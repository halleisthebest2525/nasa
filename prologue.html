PROJECT: NASARETH

In 2033, NASA launched PROJECT: GENESIS. Its mission was simple: protect Earth from a mysterious swarm of asteroids moving toward the inner solar system.

Genesis was the most advanced spacecraft humanity had ever built. Nations worked together to create its powerful detection and asteroid-deflection systems. The project cost nearly everything NASA had.

But Genesis never completed its mission. An asteroid fragment escaped detection and struck the ship. In moments, the spacecraft was torn apart.

Its emergency system launched five vital components toward different worlds across the solar system. The damaged central structure began its automatic journey back to Earth.

Only one crew member survived: the commander of Genesis, who escaped in an emergency capsule.

Two years passed. NASA recovered what remained of Genesis, but it had no money to build another ship like it. Its final hope was an older spacecraft with enough power to reach only the Moon.

Now, in 2035, that same commander has volunteered for one last mission: PROJECT: NASARETH. The five lost components must be found, but nobody knows exactly where they are. The commander must explore distant worlds, collect scraps, and upgrade the small spacecraft to travel farther.

Find all five legendary components. Rebuild Genesis. Complete the mission that once seemed lost forever.
